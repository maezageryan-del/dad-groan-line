Entry for [The One-Liner Groan-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alfasurya91-the-one-liner-groan-off-5a1febd5) on make.fun — one original dad joke, one line, 67 characters:

> I asked my roof for a loan — it said, 'I'm already over your head.'

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else. No dependencies, no network, deterministic output: the same 67-character line on every run.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Why this line

The field's visible jokes pile up on the same objects — Wi-Fi, calendars, fridges, vacuums, toasters — and recycle classics ("father figure", "folded under pressure"). The roof is a dad's domain that nobody in the 339-entry field used, and "over your head" does double work: a roof is literally over your head, and being in over your head is debt. Setup and turn inside one line, punchline in the last four words, short enough to win the size tie-break.
